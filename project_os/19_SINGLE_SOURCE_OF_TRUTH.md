8. **Rule 8 — Update Project Status & Score**: Update overall completion percentages in `project_os/01_PROJECT_STATUS.md` and `project_os/15_PROJECT_SCORE.md`.

10. **Rule 10 — Stop & Wait After Task**: Execute one task at a time. After implementing a task, run verification, update `project_os/`, and stop to report results to the user.
